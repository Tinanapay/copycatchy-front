About the CopyCatch

An academic tool focused on integrity, accuracy, and automated evaluation

1
CopyCatch is developed as a system focused on supporting academic integrity in educational environments. It is designed to assist educators in managing written submissions in a more structured and reliable way.
2
Helps detect similarities in submitted work, checks for spelling and grammar errors, and encourages originality in writing. It also supports fair evaluation practices by analyzing content quality and identifying areas that may need improvement.
3
Combines structured analysis with automated evaluation to deliver reliable and consistent results, including automated grading and report. It is built to ensure accuracy, efficiency, and clarity, making it suitable for academic use and institutional reporting!
